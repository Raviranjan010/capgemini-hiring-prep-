**Tag**: [CHAT]  
**Format**: Automated computer-based speech evaluation (Evaluation criteria: Fluency, Pronunciation, Sentence Mastery, Vocabulary).

## 1. Assessment Sub-Sections Breakdown

| Part | Section Name | What You Do | Common Mistake to Avoid | Top Pro-Tip |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Part A** | **Reading Aloud** | Read 8–10 sentences displayed on screen within 10–15 seconds each. | Speaking in a monotone, robotic cadence or rushing without pausing at punctuation. | Read with natural inflection; take a brief breath pause at commas and full stops. |
| **Part B** | **Repeat Sentences** | Listen to an audio sentence (no screen text), then repeat it verbatim after the chime. | Forgetting early words and filler stalling ("um, ah"), or fracturing the sentence midway. | Focus on the core sentence clause and mimic the speaker's rhythm and intonation. |
| **Part C** | **Sentence Completion** | Listen to an incomplete spoken sentence; state the missing 1–2 words to complete it. | Long silences while searching for an advanced vocabulary word. | State the first grammatically valid, common-sense word immediately. |
| **Part D** | **Story Retelling / Passage Summary** | Listen to a short spoken paragraph; retell the main plot points in your own words. | Trying to memorize exact verbatim wording rather than tracking key narrative facts. | Note the Situation, the Conflict, and the Final Outcome. Retell simply. |
| **Part E** | **Short Topic Speech (Extempore)** | Speak continuously for 45 seconds on an assigned prompt after 30 seconds of preparation. | Running out of things to say after 20 seconds or repeating conversational fillers. | Use the 3-step structured speaking framework (Opening, Contrast, Conclusion). |

## 2. Golden Rules for Automated Speech Engines

1. **Continuous Audio Flow (Never Pause Beyond 2.5–3 Seconds)**:  
   Automated AI speech evaluators use continuous silence detection. A silence exceeding 2.5 to 3 seconds is commonly advised to trigger an automated progression to the next question, scoring the unanswered segment as 0.
2. **The No-Self-Correction Rule**:  
   If you make a minor grammatical slip or mispronounce a word, **never stop to correct yourself** (*"She go—I mean, she went"*). Restarting or rephrasing a sentence is commonly advised to reduce fluency scores by up to 40%. Continue speaking smoothly with natural momentum.
3. **Microphone Placement**:  
   Do not position the microphone directly in front of your lips. Air bursts from plosive consonants ('P', 'B', 'T') cause audio clipping and static. Position the microphone 1 to 2 inches to the side of your mouth (near your cheek or chin).

---

## 3. The 3-Step Extempore Speaking Formula (45-Second Delivery)

- **Step 1: Opening Position (10 Seconds)**: Clearly state your perspective with a thesis statement.
- **Step 2: Contrast & Evidence (25 Seconds)**: Present two balanced supporting arguments, contrasting both sides.
- **Step 3: Conclusion (10 Seconds)**: Provide a crisp 1-sentence synthesis and wrap-up.

## 4. Frequent Speaking Topics & Model Answers

### Topic 1: Work from Home vs Working in an Office
**Opening (10s)**:  
*"In my perspective, a hybrid workplace model provides the optimal balance between individual focus and team synergy."*

**Body & Contrast (25s)**:  
*"On one hand, working remotely removes exhausting daily commutes, allowing developers to dedicate deep, uninterrupted focus to complex engineering tasks. On the other hand, in-person office sessions build spontaneous collaboration, rapid architectural brainstorming, and vital peer mentorship."*

**Conclusion (10s)**:  
*"Therefore, combining flexible remote focus days with structured in-office collaboration drives the highest sustained organizational productivity."*

## 5. Headphone & Quiet-Room Checklist
1. Use a wired USB or 3.5mm headset with a dedicated noise-canceling microphone (avoid Bluetooth latency and unexpected battery cutoffs).
2. Test and calibrate microphone input sensitivity in Windows sound settings before launching the proctored test.
3. Test in a quiet, closed room with zero background chatter, family movement, or fan buffeting.
4. Position the microphone 1 to 2 inches to the corner of your mouth and speak at a steady, conversational volume.
5. Close all background audio, voice, or recording applications (Teams, Discord, Zoom, Spotify) to prevent sound driver interference.
