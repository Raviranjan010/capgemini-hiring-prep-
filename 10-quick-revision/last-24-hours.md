# Last 24 Hours Strategy & Checklist

A structured timeline and readiness protocol for the final 24 hours before your Capgemini assessment.

## T-24 Hours to T-12 Hours: Targeted Review
- [ ] Read through [10-quick-revision/CHEATSHEET.md](CHEATSHEET.md) (5 minutes).
- [ ] Review the 10-Point Code Debugging Scanner in [04-code-debugging/README.md](../04-code-debugging/README.md).
- [ ] Review the top AI Literacy failure modes (RAG lost-in-the-middle, hybrid search, prompt injection) in [08-recent-patterns/01_ai_literacy.md](../08-recent-patterns/01_ai_literacy.md).
- [ ] Attempt one full MCQ Mock under timed conditions: [09-mock-tests/01_mcq_mock_1.md](../09-mock-tests/01_mcq_mock_1.md).

## T-12 Hours to T-4 Hours: Mental Calibration
- [ ] Review the 4 Primary Failure Modes in Debugging:
  1. Unidirectional vs Bidirectional graph edges.
  2. Premature `return` inside loop bodies.
  3. Space-optimized 0/1 Knapsack forward vs reverse traversal.
  4. Max tracker initialized to 0 with all-negative arrays.
- [ ] Practice 3 extempore speech prompts from [07-communication/01_comprehensive_english_modules.md](../07-communication/01_comprehensive_english_modules.md) using the PREP structure (Point, Reason, Example, Point).

## T-2 Hours: Environment & System Verification
- [ ] **Webcam & Microphone Check**: Confirm clean hardware input. Keep background noise below 40 dB.
- [ ] **Browser Extensions**: Disable ad blockers, translate extensions, and developer popups that trigger proctoring flags.
- [ ] **Lighting & Clean Desk**: Position lighting in front of your face; ensure no secondary monitors or phones are visible.

## During the Assessment: Section Protocols
1. **Technical MCQs**:
   - Spend maximum 45 seconds per question.
   - For pseudocode tracing, sketch state variables on scratch paper. Check loop termination bounds first.
2. **Code Debugging (20 Mins)**:
   - Click RUN immediately to catch compile errors.
   - Check input negative bounds (`Integer.MIN_VALUE`).
   - Check array indices `< n` vs `<= n`.
3. **AI-Assisted Coding (45 Mins)**:
   - Do NOT ask the bot to write full solutions repeatedly (wastes tokens).
   - Use targeted modular prompts (Prompt 1: Structure, Prompt 2: Fix edge case, Prompt 3: Optimization).
   - Always verify the bot's handling of 0, negative numbers, and boundary indices.
4. **Communication Round**:
   - Speak at steady conversational cadence (120–150 WPM).
   - Never stop speaking abruptly; use bridging phrases to conclude smoothly.
